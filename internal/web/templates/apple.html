Install the profile

Open this page on the iPhone, iPad or Mac that should get the certificate, then download the profile for "{{.DeviceName}}".

Download profile
  1. On iPhone or iPad: within 8 minutes, open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded, then tap Install in the upper-right corner.
  2. On a Mac: choose Apple menu → System Settings → General → Device Management, double-click the profile under Downloaded, then click Install.
  3. The device creates its own key and requests the certificate. The key cannot be exported.

On iPhone, if Stolen Device Protection is on and you are not in a familiar location, turn it off before you install the profile and on again afterwards.

The download works once and expires 15 minutes after you start. Back to your certificates
